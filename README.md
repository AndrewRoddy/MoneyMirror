# Money Mirror

A personal net-worth app that also accounts for what you own and what your
skills are worth in the market.

## What it does

**Financial net worth** — Track accounts, investments, and other assets alongside
loans, credit, and liabilities.

**Physical assets** — Photograph possessions (or scan a room with a short video).
The app identifies items, you confirm or correct them, and estimates resale value
from real market comparables. Confirmed items join your inventory and roll into
net worth.

**Market potential** — Upload a resume. The app builds a professional profile,
matches it to labor-market data, and shows a compensation range plus related
occupations and growth paths. This sits beside net worth — it is never added as
cash.

```
financial assets + physical assets − liabilities = estimated net worth
```
